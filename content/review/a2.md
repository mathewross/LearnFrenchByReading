# A2 review: weekend-mer, journee-difficile, chien-perdu, premier-jour-travail, fete-surprise, vieux-velo

All six: grammar natural, A2 tenses, sentences <= 14 words, appropriate content, key vocab >= 3, validator clean after fixes.

## Fixed
- weekend-mer: EN of "Ils ont mangé tout de suite" wrongly repeated the previous sentence; now "They ate right away."
- weekend-mer: "La mer est devenue grande" (EN said "rough", FR meant "big") -> "Sur la mer, les vagues sont devenues grosses." / "On the sea, the waves became big." (added vague, gros, devenues to .lex; kept "mer" at 3 occurrences).
- premier-jour-travail: "Je commençais dans une petite librairie" was awkward -> "Je commençais à travailler dans..." (EN adjusted; travailler added to .lex).
- vieux-velo: EN "like me when I was young" -> "like I did when I was young".

## Left (minor, not changed)

- chien-perdu: "Max est parti ce matin" (could be "s'est sauvé"); kept for A2 simplicity.
- vieux-velo: British spellings (tyre, pedalled) vs American elsewhere (weekend-mer uses Mom/gray); internally consistent.
- Several quoted-continuation lines are separate sentence objects (vieux-velo, chien-perdu); acceptable.
