# A1 review (9 stories)

All nine stories were read for French, English accuracy, A1 level, vocab repetition, appropriateness and glosses. All pass `build.py --check` (clean) after the fixes.

## Fixed
- lea-3: "Le kilo est dans le sac" -> "Le kilo de pommes est dans le sac" (the kilo is not literally in the bag; keeps kilo at 3 occurrences).
- lea-4: "Aujourd'hui, je suis au marché !" contradicted the setting (evening, in her hotel room) -> "Le matin, je vais au marché !" (habitual present). Added matin to .lex.
- lea-4: removed the repeated "demande sa mère" tag on two consecutive lines (the first is now a bare quoted line).
- max-2: EN "pulls out his paw" -> "pulls back his paw".
- au-restaurant: "Ils prennent la table" is unnatural -> "Ils vont à la table". Added F vont and L aller to .lex. Dropped "prendre" from key_vocab (now only 2 occurrences).
- nouveau-voisin: "J'ai un très bon café" -> "J'ai du très bon café" (natural partitive); EN "her neighbour" -> "her new neighbour".
